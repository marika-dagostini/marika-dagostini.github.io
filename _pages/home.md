### About me

I am currently a PhD student in Statistical Sciences at the [University of Bologna](https://stat.unibo.it/en), where I work under the supervision of [Massimo Ventrucci](https://www.unibo.it/sitoweb/massimo.ventrucci/en) and [Andrea Ranzi](https://www.unibo.it/sitoweb/andrea.ranzi/en). In addition to my research, I am a teaching tutor for the Bayesian Inference course led by [Daniela Cocchi](https://www.unibo.it/sitoweb/daniela.cocchi/en).

My PhD scholarship is funded by [ARPAE](https://www.arpae.it/it) within the project *“Health and Equity Co-Benefits in Support of Plans to Respond to Climate Change in Italy”*, part of the Italian National Plan for Complementary Investments of the Ministry of Health.
