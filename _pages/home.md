### About me

I am currently a PhD student in Statistical Sciences at the [University of Bologna](https://stat.unibo.it/en), where I work on Bayesian and spatial statistical methods for environmental epidemiology and public health under the supervision of [Massimo Ventrucci](https://www.unibo.it/sitoweb/massimo.ventrucci/en) and [Andrea Ranzi](https://www.unibo.it/sitoweb/andrea.ranzi/en). 

My research focuses on how statistical models can help describe, understand, and communicate the health effects of environmental and climate-related exposures, particularly when these effects vary across space and populations. I am especially interested in the role of statistics as a bridge between complex data and public health decisions, and in developing methods that are both rigorous and interpretable.

My PhD scholarship is funded by [ARPAE](https://www.arpae.it/it) within the project *“Health and Equity Co-Benefits in Support of Plans to Respond to Climate Change in Italy”*, part of the Italian National Plan for Complementary Investments of the Ministry of Health.

My academic background combines statistics, data science, geostatistics, and environmental health. Before joining the PhD programme, I completed a second-level Master's degree in *Geostatistics for Human, Animal and Environmental Health* at the University of Padua, after receiving both my Bachelor's and Master's degrees in Statistical Sciences from the University of Bologna.

In addition to my research, I am a teaching tutor for the Bayesian Inference course led by [Daniela Cocchi](https://www.unibo.it/sitoweb/daniela.cocchi/en).

This website collects my publications, research updates, and materials from conference presentations and seminars, as well as information about the projects and topics I am currently working on. I hope you enjoy looking around!
